description: "Stay signed in to more than one love.rip account."

---

Got more than one love.rip account? Stay signed in to all of them in the same browser and switch in one click. This is different from [multiple profiles](/profile/multiple-profiles), which are extra looks inside one account.

**Where to find it:** the account switcher in the sidebar, or [love.rip/dashboard/profiles](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/profiles).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add an account">
    Click **Add account** and sign in to your other account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch">
    Click **Switch** next to the account you want. The active one is marked **Active**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign out (optional)">
    Use the sign-out button next to an account to remove it from this browser. This doesn't delete the account.
  </Step>
</Steps>
