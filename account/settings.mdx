## Names

Change your **Username** and **Display name**, and manage your [aliases](/badges-and-perks/aliases).

**Where to find it:** Settings → **Names** tab.

<Note>Usernames with special characters need a special character token.</Note>

<Frame caption="The Names tab">
  <img src="/images/account-names.png" alt="Names tab with username and display name fields" />
</Frame>

## Security

Change the email on your account (you'll confirm it with a code) and set or update your password. Signed up with a social login? Set a password to also sign in with email.

**Where to find it:** Settings → **Security** tab, or [love.rip/dashboard/account?tab=security](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/account?tab=security).

<Frame caption="The Security tab">
  <img src="/images/account-security.png" alt="Security tab with email and password settings" />
</Frame>

## Connections

Link sign-in accounts: **Google**, **Discord**, **GitHub**, **Twitch**, **Kick** and **Instagram**. You can only disconnect one if you still have another way to sign in.

**Where to find it:** Settings → **Connections** tab, or [love.rip/dashboard/account?tab=connections](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/account?tab=connections).

<Tip>Link Discord to use the love.rip bot (like `/me`), enter bot giveaways and use your Discord status in the [Discord Presence widget](/links-and-widgets/widgets).</Tip>

<Frame caption="The Connections tab">
  <img src="/images/account-connections.png" alt="Connections tab with Google, Discord, GitHub, Twitch, Kick and Instagram" />
</Frame>

## Payments

Connect Stripe and sell things on your page. See [Page payments](/grow/page-payments).

**Where to find it:** Settings → **Payments** tab, or [love.rip/dashboard/account?tab=payments](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/account?tab=payments).

## Sessions

See every device signed in to your account, with its location and when it was last active. Sign out one device, or **Sign out other devices** to keep only the one you're using.

**Where to find it:** Settings → **Sessions** tab, or [love.rip/dashboard/account?tab=sessions](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/account?tab=sessions).

<Frame caption="The Sessions tab">
  <img src="/images/account-sessions.png" alt="Sessions tab listing signed-in devices" />
</Frame>

## Delete your account

**Where to find it:** Settings → **Danger zone** tab, or [love.rip/dashboard/account?tab=danger](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/account?tab=danger).

<Warning>
  Deleting your account can't be undone. Type your username to confirm, then verify it's really you.
</Warning>
