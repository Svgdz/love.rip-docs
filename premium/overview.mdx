## Free trial

Try Premium free for 7 days. Effects stay live for the 7 days, then they turn off. Each account gets one trial.

**Where to find it:** the Premium upgrade window (Sidebar → **Premium**) or the last step of onboarding. Click **Start free trial (7 days)**.

<Note>The free trial does not include alias tokens. Only paid Premium does.</Note>

## Get Premium

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the store">
    Go to [love.rip/pricing/premium](https://www.love.rip/pricing/premium), or click **Get Premium** in the upgrade window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a code (optional)">
    Got a discount or affiliate code? Enter it in the **Code** box for 5% off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check out">
    Click **Proceed to Checkout** and pay by card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>See locked options in the dashboard? They show a **Premium** tag. Clicking one opens the upgrade window.</Tip>
