Share your invite link. When friends join through it, you unlock rewards.

**Where to find it:** Account → **Invite**, or [love.rip/dashboard/account/invite](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/account/invite).

<Frame caption="The Invite page">
  <img src="/images/invite-page.png" alt="Invite page with invite link and reward tiers" />
</Frame>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share your link">
    Copy your invite code or link (`love.rip/invite/yourcode`) and send it to friends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Friends join">
    An invite counts once your friend finishes onboarding and claims a username.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim rewards">
    Hit a milestone and press **Claim**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rewards

| Successful invites | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Custom badge token |
| 10 | Profile token (an extra profile slot) |
| 25 | Free Premium |
