Grab your link or a QR code to drop in your bio, stories or server.

**Where to find it:** the **Share your Profile** button at the bottom of the sidebar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share window">
    Click **Share your Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy or open">
    Copy **Your profile URL**, or click **Open my page** to see it live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a QR code (optional)">
    Click **love.rip QR Code**. Change the colors of the dots, corner squares and corner dots, then download it as **SVG** or **PNG**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
