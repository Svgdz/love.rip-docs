<Badge color="purple">Premium</Badge>

Create your own code. Anyone who uses it gets 5% off, and you earn 5% back in **rips**.

**Where to find it:** Account → **Affiliate**, or [love.rip/dashboard/account/affiliate](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/account/affiliate).

<Note>The affiliate program is for Premium members. Without Premium, the page shows an upgrade prompt.</Note>

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick your code">
    Choose a code that's 3–16 letters and numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Share your link: `love.rip/pricing/premium?code=yourcode`. People can also type the code in the **Code** box at checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn rips">
    You get 5% of each purchase in rips. 1 rip = 1 cent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your balance

Your rips are spent at checkout on your own purchases. Your activity list shows each reward as **Earned**, **Frozen** or **Reversed**.

<Note>Topping up your balance isn't available yet.</Note>
