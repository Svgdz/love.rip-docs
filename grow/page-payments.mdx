Connect a Stripe account and add offers to your page. Visitors can pay you right from your profile.

**Where to find it:** Account → Settings → **Payments** tab, or [love.rip/dashboard/account?tab=payments](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/account?tab=payments).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Stripe">
    Follow the steps to connect your Stripe account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an offer">
    Under **Page payments**, add an offer with a title and a price in USD.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fees

| | Free | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| love.rip fee | 9% | None |

Stripe's own processing fee still applies either way.
