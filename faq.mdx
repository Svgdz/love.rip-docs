<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is love.rip free?">
    Yes. You can make a page, add links, widgets, badges and lots of effects for free. [Premium](/premium/overview) unlocks extra effects, fonts, profiles and more.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Premium a subscription?">
    No. Premium is a one-time payment of $3.99 and it's yours for life.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I try Premium first?">
    Yes. Every account gets one 7-day free trial. When it ends, Premium effects turn off. The trial doesn't include alias tokens.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's my page address?">
    `love.rip/yourusername`. You pick your username during [onboarding](/getting-started/create-account) and can change it in [Account settings](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/account).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many links can I add?">
    Up to 25.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How big can my uploads be?">
    Avatar: 2MB. Images and fonts: 10MB. Backgrounds and audio: 100MB.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between profiles, accounts and aliases?">
    **Profiles** are different looks inside one account. They all use your username, and you switch which one is live. **Aliases** are extra addresses that open a profile you choose. **Accounts** are separate love.rip logins you can switch between in the same browser.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why isn't my Discord status showing?">
    Check that your Discord Presence widget has the right User ID, or turn on **Use linked Discord account** after linking Discord in [Connections](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/account?tab=connections).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I changed my share card but Discord still shows the old one.">
    Link previews can take a few minutes to refresh after you save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I use an option? It has a lock.">
    That option needs Premium. Clicking it opens the upgrade window.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get verified?">
    Buy Verified at [love.rip/pricing/verified](https://www.love.rip/pricing/verified), or apply through a [Verification ticket](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/support/new?topic=verified).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get help?">
    Open a ticket at [love.rip/dashboard/support](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/support), or join the Discord at [discord.gg/loverip](https://discord.gg/loverip).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
