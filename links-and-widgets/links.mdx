Add up to 25 links. Pick a platform, paste your handle or URL, and it shows up with the right icon.

**Where to find it:** Sidebar → **Links**, or [love.rip/dashboard/profile/links](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/profile/links).

## Add a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a platform">
    Choose from Snapchat, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Discord, Twitch, Spotify, Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, Threads, LinkedIn, GitHub, Reddit, Pinterest, SoundCloud, Cash App, PayPal, Venmo, Patreon, Ko-fi, Steam, Roblox, Bluesky, Amazon, Shopify, Figma, Behance, Dribbble, GitLab, Bitbucket, Last.fm, Tidal, Tumblr, Letterboxd, IMDb, Quora, Signal, Intercom, Mastodon, Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Monero, or **Custom**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Enter your username or URL. Custom links also get a title and an icon: upload your own (up to 10MB) or search the Iconify icon library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Extras">
    Pick a color tint for the icon. Turn on **Show as text only** to show the destination as text that visitors copy when they click it (handy for crypto addresses or usernames).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage your links

Drag links to reorder them. Use the buttons on each link to hide/show, edit or delete it.

## Link styling

- **Monochrome:** make every icon one fill color.
- **Scale:** resize icons from 50% to 150%.
- **Effects:** add **Glow** or **Glare**.

**Where to find it:** the styling options on the Links page.
