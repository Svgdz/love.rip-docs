Design a badge that's all yours. Each custom badge uses 1 badge token.

**Where to find it:** Badges → **Your badges** tab → **Custom badge**, or [love.rip/dashboard/profile/badges](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/profile/badges).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a badge token">
    Buy one at [love.rip/pricing/badge](https://www.love.rip/pricing/badge) ($3.99 each), or earn one from [Invites](/grow/invites). You can also use **Buy badge tokens** on the Badges page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the badge">
    Click **Custom badge**, give it a name (up to 24 characters) and upload an icon (PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 2MB).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show it off">
    It appears in Your badges, where you can reorder and style it like any other badge.
  </Step>
</Steps>
