An alias is a second address for your page. If you add the alias `nickname`, then `love.rip/nickname` opens the profile you chose.

**Where to find it:** Account → Settings → **Names** tab, or [love.rip/dashboard/account](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/account).

## Add an alias

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get an alias token">
    Each alias uses 1 alias token. Buy them at [love.rip/pricing/alias](https://www.love.rip/pricing/alias) ($1.99 each).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    In the Aliases panel, enter the handle you want and pick which profile it should open.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Paid Premium includes 3 alias tokens. The free trial does not.</Note>

<Tip>Aliases are a great way to give one of your [extra profiles](/profile/multiple-profiles) its own link.</Tip>

## Gift alias tokens

You can buy alias tokens for another member. Pick **Gift** on [love.rip/pricing/alias](https://www.love.rip/pricing/alias?for=gift) and choose who gets them.
