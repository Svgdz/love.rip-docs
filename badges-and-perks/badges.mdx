Badges sit next to (or under) your username and show what you've done on love.rip.

**Where to find it:** Sidebar → **Badges**, or [love.rip/dashboard/profile/badges](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/profile/badges).

## Your badges

The **Your badges** tab lists every badge you own. Drag to reorder, give each badge its own color, or hide the ones you don't want shown.

## Badge styling

- **Monochrome:** make every badge one color.
- **Effects:** **Glow** or **Glare**.
- **Inner border** and **outer border.**
- **Animations:** move badges back and forth, left to right or right to left. Set the speed, and choose **always animate** or **pause on hover**.

## How to get badges

The **Available badges** tab shows every badge and how to get it.

| Badge | How to get it |
| --- | --- |
| **Premium** | Get [Premium](/premium/overview) |
| **Verified** | Buy it or apply. See [Verified](/badges-and-perks/verified) |
| **Donator** | Donate from the Available badges tab |
| **Gifter** | Gift Premium to someone ([love.rip/pricing/premium?for=gift](https://www.love.rip/pricing/premium?for=gift)) |
| **Bug Hunter** | Report a bug ([love.rip/dashboard/support/new?topic=bug](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/support/new?topic=bug)) |
| **Booster** | Boost the love.rip Discord server |
| **Guild Tag** | Wear the love.rip server tag on Discord |
| **Champion** | Reach the top of the views [leaderboard](https://www.love.rip/leaderboard) |
| **Views badges** | Noticed (100 views), Picked Up (500), Viral (1,000), On the Map (2,500), Headliner (5,000), Famous (10,000) |
| **Event badges** | Participant, Third, Second and Winner, from love.rip events |
| **OG** | Given to early supporters |
| **Partner** and **Featured** | Given by the love.rip team |
| **Halloween 2026** | Seasonal event badge |
| **Founder, Manager, Supervisor, Staff, Helper** | love.rip team only |

<Tip>Linked your Discord? Some badges also give you a matching role in the love.rip Discord server.</Tip>

## Donate

Support love.rip with any amount and get the **Donator** badge. Pick $5, $10, $20 or $50, or type your own amount, then check out.

**Where to find it:** Badges → **Available badges** tab → Donator.
