The Verified badge shows people it's really you. It shows ahead of the Premium badge, and you can show it in your badge row or as a check beside your name.

## Buy Verified

Verified is $9.99, paid once, yours for life. It includes the Verified badge and a Discord role for verified members.

**Where to find it:** [love.rip/pricing/verified](https://www.love.rip/pricing/verified).

## Apply for Verified

Prefer to apply? Open a Verification ticket with a link to your social media and a proof link. You can have 1 open Verification ticket at a time.

**Where to find it:** Badges → Available badges → Verified → **Apply**, or [love.rip/dashboard/support/new?topic=verified](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/support/new?topic=verified).
