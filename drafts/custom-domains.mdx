## Add a domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Enter your domain on the Domains page. A `www` version is set up for you too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DNS records">
    At your domain provider, add the three records shown: an **Ownership TXT**, a **Routing CNAME** and a **Certificate CNAME**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it to go live">
    The status moves from **Pending DNS** to **Pending SSL** to **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="DNS records for a new domain">
  <img src="/images/draft-domains-dns-records.png" alt="DNS records to add at your domain provider" />
</Frame>

## Limits and statuses

- You can have up to 3 domains. The automatic `www` version and disabled domains don't count.
- Statuses: **Active**, **Pending DNS**, **Pending SSL**, **Disabled** and **Error**.
