**Where to find it:** Customize → Appearance → **Style** tab, or [love.rip/dashboard/customize?tab=style](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/customize?tab=style).

## Colors

Pick colors for **Text**, **Secondary text**, **Username**, **Description**, **Background**, **Accent**, **Highlight** and **Footer**.

**Where to find it:** Style tab → **Colors**.

<Frame caption="Color pickers">
  <img src="/images/style-colors.png" alt="Color fields for text, username, accent and more" />
</Frame>

## Fonts

Choose a page font from the built-in list: Satoshi, Fira Code, Poppins, JetBrains Mono, Array, Velocity, Outfit, Silkscreen, Technor, Parkinsans, Chillax, Drippy and Minecraft.

**Where to find it:** Style tab → font settings.

### Custom fonts <Badge color="purple">Premium</Badge>

Upload your own font for the page and a separate one for your username. Supported files: WOFF2, WOFF, OTF and TTF, up to 10MB.

## Profile box

The profile box is the card your profile sits on. Set its color, **Card opacity** (0–100) and **Card blur** (0–40px). You can also add a banner to the top of the box and fade it out.

**Where to find it:** Style tab → **Profile Box**.

### Border

Give the box an outline: **Outline color**, **Thickness** (0–24), **Rounding** (0–80) and **Outline opacity**.

### Moving borders <Badge color="purple">Premium</Badge>

Animate the border with **Electric**, **Glow**, **Pulse** or **Rainbow**. You can apply the effect to the profile box and to other parts of your page: audio, volume, comments, badges, modules on the card, modules below, About, Skills, Games and media, and Couples.

## Individual border overrides

Give certain parts of your page their own border instead of the main one: volume, likes, comments, audio, couples, widgets/modules, About, Skills and media.

**Where to find it:** Style tab → **Individual Border Overrides**.

## Background gradient

Add a gradient behind your page with a **Start** color, an **End** color and an **Angle**.

**Where to find it:** Style tab → **Background Gradient**.
