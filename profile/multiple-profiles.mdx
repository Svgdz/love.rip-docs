Each profile keeps its own styling and links. Every profile uses your account username, so switching changes what's on your page. Want a look at its own address? Give it an [alias](/badges-and-perks/aliases).

**Where to find it:** the profile switcher in the sidebar.

| | Free | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profiles per account | 3 | 5 |

<Tip>Profile tokens give you extra slots on top of these. You can earn one from [Invites](/grow/invites).</Tip>

## Create a profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the switcher">
    Click the profile switcher in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create profile** and give it a name (up to 32 characters).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Style it">
    You're switched to the new profile right away. Customize it like normal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rename, switch or delete

- **Switch:** pick a profile from the list.
- **Rename:** click the pencil next to a profile.
- **Delete:** open **Manage profiles**. Deleting a profile removes that look and its links. Your username stays. You always keep at least one profile.
