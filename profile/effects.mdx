Everything here is on the **Effects** tab of Appearance. Locked options show a **Premium** tag. Picking one opens the upgrade.

**Where to find it:** Customize → Appearance → **Effects** tab, or [love.rip/dashboard/customize?tab=effects](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/customize?tab=effects).

  <img src="/images/customize-effects-tab.png" alt="Customize Appearance page on the Effects tab" />
</Frame>

## Background effects

Layer effects over your background: **Fuzzy**, **Blured**, **Old TV**, **Dither**, **Rain**, **Particles** and **Snowflake**. You can stack up to 6.

**Where to find it:** Effects tab → **Background Effects**.

<Frame caption="Background effects">
  <img src="/images/effects-background.png" alt="Background effects picker with stacked layers" />
</Frame>

## Animated backgrounds <Badge color="purple">Premium</Badge>

Swap your image or video background for a live animated one: Aurora, Prism, Silk, Light rays, Side rays, Prismatic burst, Pixel blast, Color bends, Floating lines, Iridescence, Plasma wave, CRT warp and Molten metal.

<Warning>An animated background replaces your video or image background.</Warning>

<Frame caption="Animated background picker">
  <img src="/images/effects-animated-background.png" alt="Animated background options like Aurora and Prism" />
</Frame>

## Username effects

Make your name pop. Stack up to 3 effects when they work together.

- **Free:** Gradient, Rainbow, Glow, Stars
- **Premium** <Badge color="purple">Premium</Badge>: Fuzzy, Typewriter, Decrypt, Flames, Shimmer, Flicker, Distort, Lock On

**Where to find it:** Effects tab → **Username Effects**.

<Frame caption="Username effects">
  <img src="/images/effects-username.png" alt="Username effects list with free and Premium options" />
</Frame>

## Bio effects

Add one effect to your bio. **Glow** is free. Every other bio effect, including **Typewriter Descriptions**, needs Premium.

**Where to find it:** Effects tab → **Bio Effects**.

<Frame caption="Bio effects">
  <img src="/images/effects-bio.png" alt="Bio effects picker" />
</Frame>

## Profile box effect

Make the profile box react to the mouse with **Tilt**, **Depth** or **Glare**.

**Where to find it:** Effects tab → **Profile Box Effect**.

<Frame caption="Profile box effect">
  <img src="/images/effects-profile-box.png" alt="Tilt, Depth and Glare options for the profile box" />
</Frame>

## Cursor trails

Leave a trail behind the mouse.

- **Free:** Hearts, Trail, Cursor Cat (a little cat that chases the cursor, with different skins)
- **Premium** <Badge color="purple">Premium</Badge>: Canvas, Snowflake, Bubble, Rainbow, Stardust, Ribbon

**Where to find it:** Effects tab → mouse trail settings.

<Frame caption="Cursor trail settings">
  <img src="/images/effects-cursor-trail.png" alt="Cursor trail options including Cursor Cat" />
</Frame>

## Click bursts and sounds <Badge color="purple">Premium</Badge>

- **Click burst:** Spark, Hearts, Sparkles or Confetti when someone clicks.
- **Click Sound Effect** and **Button Sound Effect:** pick a built-in sound (Silent, Ascend, Blip, Bubble, Click, Gun click, Pop, Sparkle) or upload your own (up to 10MB).

**Where to find it:** Effects tab → **Page Effects**.
