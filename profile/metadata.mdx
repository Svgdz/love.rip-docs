Metadata controls what people see when you paste your love.rip link somewhere, and how your browser tab looks.

**Where to find it:** Customize → **Metadata**, or [love.rip/dashboard/customize/metadata](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/customize/metadata).

<Note>Metadata is a Premium feature. It covers the custom favicon, share card, Discord embed and tab title motion.</Note>

## Open Graph share card

Set the title and description shown in link previews, and style the generated preview image. A live preview shows the embed and browser tab as you edit.

**Where to find it:** Metadata → **Open Graph** tab.

- **Embed details:** Title and Description.
- **Colors:** Overlay color and opacity, Embed color, and colors for your username, URL, likes, dislikes and views.
- **Assets:** a **Favicon** (32×32) for your browser tab and a **Card banner** (1200×630) for the share image. JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 10MB.
- **Visibility:** choose whether the image prints your Username or Display name, and toggle Avatar, Name, URL, Likes, Dislikes and Views.
- **Download PNG:** grab the generated 1200 × 630 image. It shows your last saved version.

<Tip>Link previews can take a few minutes to refresh after you save.</Tip>

## Discord embed

Design a custom card that Discord shows when your link is posted. When it's off, Discord shows the standard Open Graph embed instead. Turn it on with the toggle on the preview.

**Where to find it:** Metadata → **Discord embed** tab.

## Browser tab animation

Animate the text in your browser tab with **Typewriter**, **Scroll** or **Flashing**.

**Where to find it:** Metadata → Open Graph tab → **Browser Tab Animation**.
