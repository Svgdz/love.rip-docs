## Display name, bio, location and occupation

Set the name shown on your page, write a short bio, and add where you are and what you do. Your bio can be up to 200 characters and 10 lines. Occupation can be up to 80 characters.

**Where to find it:** General tab → **Profile Details**.

## Joined date

Show when your account was created. Pick how it reads: **Full date**, **Time since** or **Year only**.

**Where to find it:** General tab → **Profile Controls** → **Joined**.

## View counter

Show your view count on your page. Turn on **Count-up** to make the number roll up with a short animation, and set how long it runs.

**Where to find it:** General tab → **Profile Controls** → **Views**.

## Comments

Let visitors leave comments on your page. Turning comments off hides the thread but keeps the comments already saved. Turn on **Match page type** to make comments use your page's font.

**Where to find it:** General tab → **Profile Controls** → **Comments**.

## Likes and dislikes

Visitors can like or dislike your page. Choose **Show feedback publicly**, and **Show count next to the buttons** to put the numbers beside the icons instead of only in the tooltip.

**Where to find it:** General tab → **Profile Controls** → **Likes**.
