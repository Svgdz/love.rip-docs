## Enter screen

The screen visitors click through before they see your profile. Use the toggle in the section header to turn it on or off.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write your text">
    Fill in the main text and the secondary text. Each can have up to 200 characters and 5 lines. Use the toolbar for bold, italic, underline, strike, code, lists, links and images.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a style">
    Choose an **Enter screen style**: Classic, Horizontal Split, Vertical Split, Slide Up, Slide Down, Slide Left or Slide Right. Premium adds **Terminal Loading**, **Profile Intro** and **Paper Burn** <Badge color="purple">Premium</Badge>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Animate the text">
    Choose a **Text animation**: None, Fade Up, Typewriter, Decrypt, Blur In or Scale In.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set colors and backdrop">
    Pick the **Primary Text Color**, **Secondary Text Color** and **Accent Color**. Then set **Backdrop Opacity** (0–100%), **Backdrop Blur** (0–40px) and **Animation Speed** (0.5×–2×).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Hit **Save** in the bar at the bottom.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Profile load style

Choose how your profile box and content animate in after the enter screen. Set the **Entrance effect** and its **Animation speed** (0.5×–2×).

- **Free:** Bloom In, Focus In
- **Premium** <Badge color="purple">Premium</Badge>: Float Up, Sweep Reveal, Elastic, Digital Glitch, Motion Blur, Card Deal
