Your uploads live on the **General** tab of Appearance, in the Assets area.

**Where to find it:** Customize → Appearance → **General** tab, or [love.rip/dashboard/customize](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/customize).

## Avatar and decoration

Upload a profile picture, then pick an avatar decoration to frame it. You can hide the avatar with **Show avatar** and adjust **Corner roundness**.

<Note>Avatars can be JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 2MB.</Note>

## Backgrounds

Add an image or video behind your page. With more than one, you can **Shuffle** them, **Loop** them and set the **Hold time** for each. Drag to reorder.

| | Free | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background slots | 1 | 8 |

<Note>
  Images: JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF. Videos: MP4 or WebM. Backgrounds can be up to 100MB.
</Note>

## Music player

Add songs that play on your page. Drag tracks to reorder them.

| | Free | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audio tracks | 1 | 5 |

Player options:
- **Player style:** Normal or Lyrics (synced lyrics). With Lyrics, pick the **Lyrics position** (Below or Side) and **Lyrics alignment** (Left, Center or Right).
- **Bar layout:** Compact or Wide.
- **Cover shape:** Rounded square or Circle.
- **Blurred cover**, **Shuffle**, **Loop**, **Show player** and **Volume knob** toggles.
- **Starting volume.**

<Note>Audio can be MP3, OGG, WAV or WebM, up to 100MB per file.</Note>
