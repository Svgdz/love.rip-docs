## Layout presets

Choose the overall shape of your page: **Classic**, **Modern**, **Split** or **Floating**.

**Where to find it:** Customize → Appearance → **Layout** tab, or [love.rip/dashboard/customize?tab=layout](https://www.love.rip/dashboard/customize?tab=layout).

## Alignment and placement

Fine-tune where things sit:

- **Links alignment:** Left, Center or Right.
- **Bio alignment:** Left or Center (Modern layout only).
- **Badges:** Next to username or Below username (Classic and Floating layouts only).
- **Widgets:** Above links or Below links.

**Where to find it:** Customize → Appearance → **Layout** tab.
