## Sign up

Make a free account with email or a social login. Once you're in, you'll go straight to setup.

**Where to find it:** the sign-up page at [love.rip/sign-up](https://www.love.rip/sign-up). Already have an account? Use [love.rip/sign-in](https://www.love.rip/sign-in).

## Finish onboarding

Setup has 4 short steps. You can leave and come back. Your progress stays put.

**Where to find it:** [love.rip/onboarding](https://www.love.rip/onboarding) (you're sent here automatically after signing up).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick your names">
    Enter a **Display name** and a **Username**. Your username becomes your page address: `love.rip/yourname`. Usernames are lowercase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us how you found love.rip">
    Pick one option. If you choose "Other", type where.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how you'll use it">
    Pick the option that fits you best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Premium (optional)">
    Start the 7-day free trial, buy Premium, or skip and stay on the free plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a friend sent you an invite link, just sign up through it. Your join counts for them once you finish onboarding with a username. More in [Invites](/grow/invites).
